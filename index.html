Learn by doing

Your beginner roadmap

Build skills in a sensible order instead of trying every AI tool at once.

1

Learn how prompts work

State your goal, give context, describe the format you need, and ask for revisions.

2

Choose one useful tool

Start with one tool that solves a real task. Check its free-plan limits before signing up.

3

Make something helpful

Create a product description, a study guide, a simple website or a business advert.

4

Get feedback and improve

Show your work to potential users, learn what they need, and improve your next version.
